Use **Budget** in the sidebar to set a monthly spending limit for each category and review spending for the selected month. The limits are saved with the open portfolio.

## Set category limits

1. Open **Budget**.
2. Enter a category, or choose one of the suggested categories.
3. Enter a positive monthly limit and click **Add budget**.

You can add categories that have no transactions yet. To budget a category already appearing under **Spending without a budget**, click **Set a limit** to fill in its name, enter the limit, and add it. To change or remove a limit, remove the category with its trash button; add it again to set a new amount.

## Read the monthly summary

Use the arrows beside the month to move between months. The summary shows total spending, the sum of all category limits, the amount remaining, and overall progress. Each budgeted category shows its spending against its limit and how much is left. Spending over a limit is highlighted; categories without a limit appear separately under **Spending without a budget**.

The same category limits are used for every month you view; selecting another month changes the spending totals, not the limits.

## What counts as spending

The page totals expense transactions for the selected month by category. Transfers and investment transactions are excluded. Transactions without a category are grouped under **Uncategorized**. Amounts are converted using account exchange rates into the budget currency.

The budget currency starts as the app currency when you first save a limit. Existing limits remain in their saved currency if you later change the app's reporting currency.

## Dashboard

Add **Budget Health** from the dashboard's **Customize** mode to see current-month spending against the total budget, overall progress, and any categories over their limits.
