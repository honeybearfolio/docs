HoneyBear Folio can track debts that do not live in a cash account. The feature appears in the sidebar as **Liabilities**. Each liability has a balance history, and the latest balance is included in net worth.

## What You Can Track

Each liability has a name, category, optional currency, and optional notes. The categories are:

- Mortgage
- Auto loan
- Credit card
- Student loan
- Personal loan
- Other

## Adding a Liability

1. Open **Liabilities** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Liability**.
3. Enter a name.
4. Choose a category.
5. Optionally choose a currency. If you leave it empty, the liability uses the app currency.
6. Optionally add notes.
7. Save the liability.

## Recording Balances

Open a liability's **Balance History** and add a dated balance. You can create, edit, and delete those records. The latest balance is the one counted in net worth.

Balances can be converted into your app currency. If a currency has no rate yet, set one under **Settings → General → Exchange Rates**, or let the app fetch it from Yahoo Finance.
