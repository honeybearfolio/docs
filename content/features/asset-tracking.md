HoneyBear Folio can track assets that do not live in a brokerage or cash account. The feature appears in the sidebar as **Asset Tracker** and is designed for manual inventory and valuation history.

## What You Can Track

Each asset has a name, category, optional currency, and optional notes. The app currently supports these categories:

- Real estate
- Vehicle
- Jewelry
- Art
- Collectible
- Other

This makes it suitable for assets such as a home, car, watch collection, artwork, or any other item you want included in your overall financial picture.

## Adding an Asset

1. Open **Asset Tracker** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Asset**.
3. Enter a name.
4. Choose a category.
5. Optionally choose a currency.
6. Optionally add notes.
7. Save the asset.

If you leave the currency empty, the asset uses the app's default currency.

## Recording Valuations

Assets become useful once you add valuation entries.

For each asset, you can open **Value History** and add dated valuations. Each valuation stores:

- A date
- A numeric value

You can create, edit, and delete valuations. The latest valuation is shown in the main asset list, and older entries remain available in the history panel.

## Currency Conversion and Net Worth

Asset values can be converted into your selected app currency when HoneyBear Folio calculates totals. This lets you keep assets in their original currency while still viewing a combined net worth.

If your assets use multiple currencies, make sure your exchange rates are configured in the app. Without matching rates, converted totals may be incomplete or inaccurate.
