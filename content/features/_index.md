HoneyBear Folio keeps accounts, investments, assets, and debts in one local database, and gives you charts and tools for reading that file.

## Guides

- [Dashboard](dashboard/): Arrange the cards, and give any card its own date range and filters.
- [Goals](goals/): Track a target funded by accounts and investments.
- [Import & Export](import-export/): Bring in CSV, Excel, or JSON, and send data back out, including a PDF report for a period you choose.
- [Rules](rules/): Match a payee by text or by regular expression and fill in the category, account, notes, tags, and any other field you choose. Rules run while you type a transaction and again during import.
- [Scheduled Transactions](scheduled-transactions/): Recurring income, bills, and investments. They stay pending until you apply them.
- [Asset Tracking](asset-tracking/): A value history for a home, a car, or anything else you own.
- [Liabilities](liabilities/): A balance history for mortgages, loans, and other debts.
- [AI Assistant](ai-agents/): An optional local [Ollama](https://ollama.com) model that can look up accounts, transactions, holdings, assets, liabilities, and goals.

## Also in the app

**Investments.** Holdings, cost basis, and gain or loss. Quotes and daily prices come from Yahoo Finance and feed the allocation chart and the performance heatmap.

**Transactions.** Cash accounts, transfers between accounts, and brokerage trades. A buy or sell records the share leg and the matching cash leg. Search, edit, duplicate, and delete from the account.

**FIRE calculator.** Open **FIRE Calculator** in the sidebar. It starts from your balances, spending, and savings, then projects a target with a single path and a Monte Carlo range.

**More than one database.** Create a file, open an existing one, and switch from the sidebar. The sidebar shows which file is open.

**Currencies.** Accounts and transactions can use different currencies. Exchange rates come from Yahoo Finance. In **Settings → General**, any rate can be replaced with one you enter.

**Privacy mode.** Masks amounts and leaves the currency symbol visible.

**Language and appearance.** The interface is available in English and Spanish. Choose light, dark, high contrast, ink, or follow the system, and scale the type size, under **Settings → Customization**.
