The dashboard opens with cards for net worth, account and transaction counts, income against expenses, cash flow, how assets are split, and spending by category. The toolbar sets the date range for the page and which accounts are included. Hide an account when you want a narrower view.

## Arranging cards

Click **Customize**. While that mode is on you can:

- Drag a card by its handle to a new place on the grid.
- Resize a card from its corner.
- Remove a card.
- Choose **Add card** to place another copy of any card.
- Click **Reset layout** to restore the default order and sizes. The date range and the account filter stay as they are.

Click **Done** to leave customize mode. If every card has been removed, **Customize** is the way to add one back.

The arrangement is saved on this computer. Copying the database file does not copy it.

## A range and filters for one card

Each card can follow the toolbar, or use its own settings. Open the card's filters while customizing.

**Date range.** Choose **Follow dashboard**, or pick 1M, 3M, 6M, YTD, 1Y, All, or a custom start and end.

**Filters.** Add one or more conditions. A transaction is included only when every condition matches. The fields are account, category, payee, notes, amount, currency, ticker, tags, fee, shares, price, and date. Text fields can match exactly, by a fragment, or by a regular expression. Number and date fields can match an exact value or a greater-than or less-than comparison.

An **equals** condition on account shows that account on the card even when the toolbar hides it. **Does not equal** removes an account from the card.
